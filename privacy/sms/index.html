Gude Capital SMS Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026. This policy covers SMS/text messaging from Gude Capital (Gude Capital Management, LP) ("Gude Capital," "we," "us") on our Microsoft Teams Calling Plan phone numbers.

1. Information we collect

When you exchange SMS messages with a Gude Capital Teams phone number, we receive your mobile phone number, the content of your messages, and related delivery metadata needed to send and receive texts.

2. How we use information

We use this information only for one-to-one business conversations with you — for example scheduling, project coordination, and responding to your questions. Message frequency varies based on those conversations.

3. Mobile opt-in data will not be shared with third parties

Mobile opt-in information will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile opt-in data will not be shared with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, opt-in status, or SMS message content with third parties for their marketing or any other purpose unrelated to delivering the SMS conversation. Microsoft and our mobile carriers may process messages solely to operate and deliver the messaging service.

4. Opt-in, HELP, and opt-out

You may opt in by texting a Gude Capital number, by verbal consent on a call, or by providing your number and consent in writing. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. No further messages will be sent after you reply STOP (except a confirmation of the opt-out).

5. How to contact us

Privacy and SMS support: agude@gudecapital.com. See also our SMS Terms and Conditions and the general website Privacy Policy.
