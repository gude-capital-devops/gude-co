Service Tiers

From early design validation to full construction document review — choose the scope that fits where your project is today.

01

Preliminary Plan Review

Early-phase review to inform design development before you invest in full construction documents.

  • Use and occupancy classification
  • Type of construction & height/area
  • Means of egress & fire-resistance
  • Foundation design concepts
02

Complete Plan Review

Comprehensive review of design drawings and specifications for all requested disciplines.

  • Detailed code compliance checklist
  • Documented violations & corrections
  • Follow-up consultation during construction
  • Complimentary re-review of reissued plans
03

Limited-Scope & Consulting

Targeted technical review or code interpretation for specific building components or applications.

  • Single-discipline or partial packages
  • Code evaluation & special studies
  • Peer review of third-party submissions
  • Pre-submittal compliance guidance
