Engineering manifesto (Pacific Outdoor Living)

Pacific has completed 20,000+ projects on a design-build promise: on budget, one coordinated team from site analysis through construction. Their permitting department, in-house crews, and five-step design process require structural engineering that keeps pace with production.

Dixon Engineering established the Gude Pacific Structural Library: a detail corpus drawn from Pacific's construction standards and estimating library, converted into governed blocks and paired calculation packages. Details are indexed by system (pergola, patio cover, pool-adjacent structure) and by element (post base, beam, brace, connection) so designers select the correct assembly from the library on every job.

Calculation packages follow the same taxonomy. A freestanding pergola system includes member-level checks, connection supplements, and paired construction details. A patio cover follows the same internal review path before authority submittal. Each project starts from a defined engineering baseline.

That is the licensed engineering model Dixon delivers: systems-based structural engineering for high-volume design-build firms.
