Accessibility

Effective August 16, 2026. Gude intends the Sites (gude.co, plans.gude.co, dixon.gude.co, and evaluation.gude.co) to be usable by people with disabilities.

We aim to meet the spirit of WCAG 2.2 Level AA: readable text, keyboard access, labels on forms, and alternatives for non-text content where we can. The Sites are simple HTML. If something blocks you, tell us.

Request help

Email gos@gudecapital.com with the page URL and what you were trying to do. We will work with you on an alternative — including sending the same information by email or another format we can reasonably provide.

This page is a baseline. We can expand it if a jurisdiction or client needs a longer statement.
